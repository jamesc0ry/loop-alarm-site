Loop Alarm for Apple Watch

Privacy Policy

Effective August 12, 2026

Loop Alarm is designed to schedule reminders on your Apple Watch without collecting your personal data.

On this page

Information stored by the app

Loop Alarm stores the reminder on or off state, the selected minute interval or hourly minute, and the next scheduled reminder time in local Apple Watch storage. The app uses this information only to restore your settings, schedule the reminder, and show its current status.

Data collection

Loop Alarm does not collect personal data. The app does not send its settings or usage information off your device. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, network service, or third-party software development kit.

Sharing, selling, and tracking

Loop Alarm does not share or sell data and does not track you across apps or websites.

Retention and your controls

Your reminder settings remain in the app's local storage while you use Loop Alarm. Changing a reminder or turning reminders off updates that local state. watchOS manages the app's local storage when the app is removed.

This website

This static site is hosted with GitHub Pages. The site does not add analytics, forms, tracking scripts, advertising, or cookies through site code. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's Pages documentation for details.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if Loop Alarm's data practices change. A revised policy will show a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email Loop Alarm Support at loopalarm.help@outlook.com.
