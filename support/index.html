Common questions

Using Loop Alarm

How do I schedule a reminder?

Open Loop Alarm and use the Digital Crown to choose 1 to 59 minutes. Continue to the hourly option to choose a specific minute of every hour, then turn reminders on.

Why did reminders stop after an alert?

When an alert appears while Loop Alarm is open, choose Continue to schedule the next reminder. If an alert finishes without being acknowledged, the reminder loop ends.

Why does the app say it is scheduling?

watchOS requires Loop Alarm to be in the foreground when it arms a reminder. Open the app and leave it visible briefly so scheduling can finish.

How do I stop reminders?

Turn reminders off on the main screen. You can also choose Stop reminders when an alert is visible.
